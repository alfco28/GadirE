# Contributing to GadirE

Thank you for your interest in helping us improve GadirE, the interactive map of the University of Cádiz. GadirE is being built to make university spaces and services easier to find, use and share. Every report, suggestion and contribution helps us make the map more accurate and useful for the university community.

The project is still in a preliminary stage, so you may find missing information, mistakes or unfinished features. Please let us know when you do.

## Report a missing place

If a place, service or point of interest is not shown on the map, please use the **[missing place form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdgbuxXkLoHbwkBDh5i6TF-OzIHC7WK_Y6LDy4tCTiSR3Sg5Q/viewform)**.

When possible, include the name of the place, its campus, building and floor, as well as any other information that could help us locate and identify it. You can also open this form by selecting an empty area of the map.

## Report incorrect information

If a place is already on the map but its information is wrong or out of date, please use the **[incorrect information form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdEZjtvKMVmpLC3zcajTqxQdqtVs6yuITLAooP6FPez8nhKoQ/viewform)**.

Please describe what is incorrect and, where possible, provide the correct information. Examples include:

- An incorrect name, address, building or floor.
- A marker in the wrong position.
- An inaccurate or incomplete description.
- A service that has moved, closed or changed its opening arrangements.

## Report a bug

For problems that are not about a particular place, please report them by email at **[t.digital.ceuca@uca.es](mailto:t.digital.ceuca@uca.es)**. This includes issues such as:

- The map, search, filters or menus not working as expected.
- Problems on a particular browser, device or screen size.
- Calendar or news content failing to load.
- Accessibility, language or display problems.
- Broken links, installation problems or repeated error messages.

To help us investigate, please include a short description of what happened, what you expected to happen, the steps needed to reproduce the problem and, if relevant, your browser, device and operating system. Screenshots or a link to the affected place can be useful too. Please do not include personal or sensitive information in a report.

## Suggest an improvement or a new idea

Ideas are welcome, whether they concern the map, the information it provides or the way people use it. You can suggest a new feature, an improvement to an existing workflow, a new data source, a translation, or any other idea by emailing **[t.digital.ceuca@uca.es](mailto:t.digital.ceuca@uca.es)**.

It is helpful to explain:

- What you would like to improve.
- Who would benefit from it.
- How you imagine it working.
- Any examples or related services that inspired the idea.

Ideas do not need to be fully developed before you share them. A clear description of the problem is often the best starting point.

## Contribute to the project

The source code is available on **[GitHub](https://github.com/alfco28/GadirE)**. It is publicly available for reference and collaboration, although the project is not distributed as free software. Please read the repository's licence and contribution conditions before reusing, redistributing or adapting the code.

Depending on the change, useful contributions may include:

- Improving the interface or making it more accessible.
- Improving translations and wording.
- Checking map data and other content.
- Fixing a bug or improving performance.
- Improving documentation.
- Testing the application on different devices, browsers and campuses.
- Reviewing a proposed change and sharing constructive feedback.

Before making a substantial code change, please contact us first so that we can discuss the approach and avoid duplicated work. Keep changes focused, preserve existing attributions and licences, and test the affected feature before submitting it.

## Other ways to help

You can also contribute by:

- Sharing GadirE with students and staff who may find it useful.
- Helping someone use the map or install it as a progressive web app.
- Checking whether information is still accurate after a building or service changes.
- Sharing feedback from your campus or faculty.
- Pointing us towards reliable public sources of university information.

## A few guidelines

Please be respectful and considerate when reporting issues or discussing ideas. Report one issue per form where possible, use a descriptive subject in emails, and distinguish clearly between confirmed information and suggestions or assumptions.

Do not submit passwords, personal identification numbers, private contact details or other confidential information. If a report includes a screenshot, check it carefully and remove anything that should not be shared.

Thank you for helping GadirE become a more accurate, accessible and useful resource for everyone.
